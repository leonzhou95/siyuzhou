Siyu Zhou, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor in International Journalism and Communication

Cheung Kong School of Journalism and Communication, Shantou University

I completed my first PhD in Communication at Universitat Pompeu Fabra (Spain) in 2022, examining how the Chinese diaspora in Spain used digital platforms to perform identity and accumulate social capital across national and cultural boundaries. Since then, my work has developed along two related lines: one continues this interest in how mobile populations use digital platforms for identity performance, and the other examines organisational platforms — particularly enterprise social media and generative AI — across the boundary between work and everyday life. I work primarily through qualitative case research, complemented by survey and computational text analysis.
